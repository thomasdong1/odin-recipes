Lasagna

Delicious slice of Lasagna

This classic beef lasagna uses ready-to-bake sheets to keep preparation quick and straightforward.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb (450g) ground beef
  • 2 Cups Marinara Sauce
  • 15 oz (425g) ricotta cheese
  • 10 Lasagna Noodles

Steps

  1. Cook Beef
  2. Assemble Lasagna
  3. Bake for 30 min
  4. Serve!
